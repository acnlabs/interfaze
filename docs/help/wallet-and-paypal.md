# Credits & PayPal (interfaze.io)

Public help source for **Add Credits**. Keep aligned with `/help/wallet`.

## Credits

- **100 Credits = $1** face value in USD.
- Credits pay for billable chat usage and related platform charges on your signed-in account.

## Checkout

- Top-up runs through **PayPal** (log in to PayPal or pay with debit/credit card on PayPal’s page).
- You receive the **face amount in Credits**. PayPal’s **channel fee** is charged on top (shown before you pay). It is not an Interfaze markup.

## If PayPal declines payment

- Declines are decided by **PayPal** or your card issuer. Interfaze starts the order but does not see the detailed reason.
- Try another PayPal account or card allowed for USD payments, complete PayPal verification, or contact PayPal with the reference on their page.
- Avoid many rapid retries — that can increase blocks.

## After a successful payment

Check **Wallet → Recent activity** in Interfaze. If capture succeeded but balance looks wrong, contact support with the time and amount.
